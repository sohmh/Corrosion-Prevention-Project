## 📁 04 Raw

| Element | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | To serve as the **temporary working space** for raw data that has *just been collected* but has **not yet been verified** by a second team member or checked against experimental logs. |
| **Key Workflow** | **Data Entry:** All measured data (e.g., initial $\text{pH}$, initial mass logs) is first uploaded here. **Verification:** Once the data is confirmed as accurate and complete, it must be **moved** to the final archive folder: `03_Data/Raw/`. |
| **Example Contents** | • $\text{Initial_Masses_Temp.csv}$ (Pending verification)<br>• $\text{Daily_Log_Oct01.csv}$ (Daily $\text{pH}$ and $\text{Temp}$ reading, awaiting final check)<br>• Unedited instrument $\text{CSV}$ files directly from the scale/meter. |
| **Related Tasks** | $\text{Data Logging}$, $\text{Solution Preparation}$, $\text{Sample Measurement}$ |
